About SHEMA

Bridging Kerala & Shepparton

കേരളവും ഷെപ്പാർട്ടണും തമ്മിൽ

Shepparton Malayalee Association (SHEMA) Inc. is an incorporated community organisation representing Malayalee families in and around Shepparton. Since 2016 we have worked to preserve our cultural identity, support community connection and celebrate Kerala's traditions through cultural events, festivals and social gatherings. Many of our families contribute to the region, particularly in healthcare: nursing, aged care and allied health. We are proud to have the support of Cr Shane Sali, Mayor of Greater Shepparton.

What SHEMA does locally

  • Runs 12 cultural events each year, including Onam, a talent show and an athletics day.
  • Brings 350+ Malayalee families together for festivals and social gatherings.
  • Preserves cultural identity and celebrates Kerala's traditions.
  • Contributes to the broader Greater Shepparton community, strengthening multicultural harmony, wellbeing and local development.

Describe this scene

A stylised 3D model. In the centre stands a brass nilavilakku, a traditional Kerala oil lamp, with a round base, a slender stem, a shallow bowl holding five wicks, and a pointed spire. It stands in the middle of a pookalam, a circular flower carpet in rings of red, gold, cream, green and ochre. To the front left is an Onam sadhya served on a banana leaf: a mound of rice with small servings of curry beside it. To the back right is a chenda, a tall wooden drum with rope lacing and two sticks resting on top. When the lamp is lit, a small flame burns at each of the five wicks.

Kerala heritage

Light the lamp

കേരളത്തിന്റെ പൈതൃകം

In Kerala, a nilavilakku is lit to open a celebration. Light ours, turn it around, and choose a numbered point to see what each part means.

  1. 1

    The flame

    A nilavilakku is lit at the start of festivals, ceremonies and community gatherings in Kerala. Press "Light the lamp" to light this one.

    Temple festivals

  2. 2

    Bell metal and brass

    Nilavilakku means "floor lamp" in Malayalam. The lamps are traditionally cast in bell metal or brass, part of Kerala's long metal-craft tradition.

    Kerala handicrafts

  3. 3

    Pookalam

    A pookalam is a carpet of fresh flower petals, laid ring by ring at the entrance of homes during the ten days of Onam.

    Pookalam

  4. 4

    Onam Sadhya

    The Onam feast is vegetarian and served on a banana leaf: rice in the middle, with curries, pickles and sweets set around it.

    Onam Sadhya

  5. 5

    Chenda

    The chenda is a tall wooden drum played with sticks. A chenda melam, a full percussion ensemble, is the sound of Kerala's temple festivals.

    Chenda Melam
